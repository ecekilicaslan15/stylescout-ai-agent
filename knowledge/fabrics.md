# Fabrics Guide

Concise reference for common fashion fabrics used in StyleScout styling decisions.

## Linen

Linen is a lightweight, breathable natural fabric made from flax fibers. It feels cool against the skin and dries quickly, which makes it ideal for hot and humid weather. Linen is commonly used for summer shirts, relaxed trousers, dresses, and lightweight blazers. It wrinkles easily, so steam or light ironing is often needed. Machine wash on a gentle cycle with cold water and air dry when possible to preserve fiber strength.

## Cotton

Cotton is a soft, breathable natural fabric that is comfortable in warm weather and easy to wear every day. It absorbs moisture well and works for t-shirts, casual shirts, jeans, skirts, and lightweight layers. Cotton is versatile across spring and summer but can feel heavy when very thick. Wash in cold or warm water depending on garment construction, and avoid high heat when drying to reduce shrinkage.

## Wool

Wool is a warm, insulating natural fabric well suited to cold weather and winter outerwear. It retains heat effectively and is commonly used for coats, sweaters, blazers, and tailored trousers. Fine wool can feel breathable while still providing warmth, but heavy wool is best for low temperatures. Dry cleaning or hand washing is often recommended for structured wool garments to prevent shrinkage and maintain shape.

## Silk

Silk is a smooth, luxurious natural fabric with a soft drape and subtle sheen. It is used for blouses, dresses, scarves, eveningwear, and elegant linings. Silk is sensitive to heat, water, and friction, so it usually needs delicate care such as hand washing or professional dry cleaning. Store silk away from direct sunlight and avoid harsh detergents to protect the fibers.

## Polyester

Polyester is a durable synthetic fabric that resists wrinkles and dries quickly. It is often blended with natural fibers to improve shape retention and affordability. Polyester works well for activewear, outer shells, travel clothing, and easy-care everyday pieces. It is less breathable than linen or cotton in hot weather, but it can be useful for layered outfits and rainy conditions. Machine wash on a gentle cycle and tumble dry on low heat unless garment labels specify otherwise.
